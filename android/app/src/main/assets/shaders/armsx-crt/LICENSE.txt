Why they are original rather than bundled from libretro
------------------------------------------------------
The obvious source for CRT presets is libretro's `slang-shaders`, and it is a superb
collection. It is also a MIXED-LICENCE work — most of the CRT shaders in it are
GPL-2.0-or-later, some are public domain, and a few carry no clear header at all.
ARMSX is proprietary. Redistributing GPL shader source inside a proprietary app is
defensible (they are data interpreted at runtime, not linked code, so they do not reach
the emulator's own licence) but it obliges us to ship each licence text, preserve every
header, and re-audit the set on every update — for a handful of presets whose job is to
make the app look right on first run.

Writing them instead removes the question. There are no third-party obligations attached
to anything in this folder.

Third-party shaders are still fully supported — they are just not REDISTRIBUTED. The
in-app shader manager (Settings > Shaders, or the in-game Graphics pane) downloads the
full libretro `slang-shaders` collection, and imports any `.zip` or folder of shaders the
user already has. Those are the user's own copy under their own terms; ARMSX only runs
them.

Runtime
-------
Rendered by librashader (MPL-2.0, see third_party/librashader/NOTICE), which requires the
Vulkan present backend — set [video] gpu_backend = "vulkan" in settings.toml, or pick
Vulkan in the renderer settings.
