omitted unsupported/placeholder groups; added conservative title/region/serial index.
Serial metadata is derived from the bundled ps1-cover-titles.tsv; its source commit
and attribution are recorded in ps1-cover-titles-NOTICE.txt. No inferred ROM identity
is used: this index maps known serials to cheat lists, not filenames to serials.
